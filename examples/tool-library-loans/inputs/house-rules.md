# Lantern Lane Tool Library: house rules (extract)

> Fictional demo. Lantern Lane Tool Library, its people and its data are invented for the aidev-po worked example.

Supplied by the coordinator at intake, 2026-10-05. Copied as given.

1. Membership is yearly and pay-what-you-can.
2. Standard loans are for seven days. Ask at the desk if you need longer.
3. Power tools need a refundable cash deposit of £10 to £40, shown on the tool's card.
4. Please bring tools back clean. Tell the desk about any damage; we would rather know.
5. Three late returns in a year and you lose borrowing for a month.
6. Reservations: tell the desk and we will try to keep the tool for you.
